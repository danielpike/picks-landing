Questions

Good Questions. Real Answers.

When does Picks launch?

We are targeting the 2026 NFL season kickoff in September. That is the biggest prediction moment in sports and exactly when Picks should hit. Join the waitlist and you will be first through the door.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Picks is free. Post picks, follow other callers, build your receipts, and ask the community. No paywall, no coins, no nonsense. Just the platform.

Is this a betting app?

No. Picks is a social platform for sports predictions and discussion. No money changes hands. No bets are placed. You post your call before the game, it gets locked, and your record speaks for itself. Think ESPN debate culture meets social media.

What sports are supported?

All of them. NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, MLS, UFC, golf, fantasy lineups, player props, season predictions. If you have a take on it, Picks is where it lives.

What is the Called Shot?

The Called Shot is a long-range prediction posted significantly before the event. The Super Bowl winner called in August. The MVP picked before the season. When it hits, it is permanently badged on your profile as proof. Named after Babe Ruth pointing to center field.

Can I delete a bad pick?

No. And that is the whole point. Once you post a pick it is permanent. No edits, no deletes, no exceptions. That is what makes the big wins mean something on Picks. Your record is real because it cannot be rewritten.
